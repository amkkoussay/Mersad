# MIRSAD — Brand

## The name
**مِرصاد (Mirsad)** is Arabic for an observatory / lookout — the tower
that watches and records. The product is exactly that: a watchtower over
the memory of your AI agents. Every fact they write, every decision they
take, is observed, timestamped and kept — nothing is silently lost.

## The mark (adopted 2026-11-13 — the owner's chosen icon)
A monochrome geometric arch in off-white `#F4F5F7` on night `#0B101A`:
an outer **shared-space arch** (the one roof every agent lives under),
a **central pillar / open record** at its heart (the decision, kept on
record), and **two side panels framing the center** (the sides of a
disagreement, held open until the owner rules). No eye, no radar, no
Latin letter — an Arabic-rooted abstract sign. The icon carries NO gold:
gold stays a product signal (decisions, live writes), never decoration.

Files: `assets/icon.svg` (the official icon, also served as
`assets/logo-mark.svg`), `assets/logo-lockup.svg` (icon + wordmark),
`assets/banner.svg` (GitHub header).

## Palette (two bases, one accent — always)
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night (base) | `#0B1220` | backgrounds, the sky |
| Royal stone (base) | `#14264A` / `#0E1830` | surfaces, the tower |
| Lamp gold (the one accent) | `#E8C268` | the light, decisions, calls to attention — nothing else |
| Parchment ink | `#DBE7F5` | primary text |
| Mist blue | `#8FC1F0` / `#8BA3C2` | entities, secondary text, edges |

Rule: gold is a *signal*, not a decoration. If everything is gold,
nothing is. No second loud colour, ever; the only exception is the
conflict red `#E0655F`, used solely to flag an open conflict.

## Voice
Calm, watchful, exact. MIRSAD reports what it recorded and stays silent
about what it did not. Short sentences. No hype words, no invented
numbers — the ledger speaks.

Taglines:
- EN: **“One memory for every AI agent.”**
- AR: **«ذاكرة واحدة لكلّ وكلائك»** / «مِرصاد — مرصد ذاكرة وكلائك»

## Don'ts
- Don't recolor the mark outside the palette, or add gradients to it.
- The mark carries no gold — don't add gold, a second accent, or a glow to it; gold stays a product signal (decisions, live writes), never part of the mark.
- Don't pair the mark with busy photography; it lives on night blue.
